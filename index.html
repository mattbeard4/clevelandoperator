01 — The Master Angle

The mission, in one line.

The wheel spin is how Justin got picked. The clock is why people watch. The deal is the trophy. The diagnostic is the lesson.

› Master Statement
"48 hours. One city I've never been to. One operator who needs his business fixed. Go."
› What makes this different from V1 (Florida w/ Joe)
Justin already runs the model. He's done deals. The story isn't rebuild. It's find the leak. His machine works — it's just sputtering. Most viewers aren't broken either. Justin is their mirror.
02 — The Main Plot

Five acts. One ticking clock.

Tight arc, escalating stakes, no slack. Each act has its own job. The clock is visible the entire video.

01
The Setup
The Wheel Spins. The Clock Starts.
0:00 — 1:30 · CLOCK STARTS 48:00:00
Cold open: fast-cut montage of the wheel spinning in front of the community, Justin's name landing, his face on the reveal call. Hard cut to me on the plane: "In 48 hours I have to land in a city I've never been to, fix his business, and try to do a deal — all on camera." Clock starts on screen.
02
The Landing
Boots Down. Drive to Akron.
1:30 — 4:00 · CLOCK ~46:00:00
Red-eye lands 7:30 AM Friday. Tired, real, no makeup. Drive to Akron — narrate over the windshield: who Justin is, what he's already done with the model, what's NOT clicking. Set the diagnostic up. "His machine works. It's just sputtering. My job in 48 hours is to find the leak and prove the fix."
03
The Diagnostic
Whiteboard. Find the Leak.
4:00 — 8:00 · CLOCK ~44:00:00
Arrive at Justin's. Hug, quick reaction. Then straight to the whiteboard. Pull his actual numbers on camera — messages, Tier 1s, TTBs, offers. Find the leak live. This is the teaching heart of the video — non-RE viewers learn the Gatekeeper model in 2 minutes, RE operators see their own business in Justin's numbers.
04
The Run
I Take the Controls. Stakes Escalate.
8:00 — 14:00 · CLOCK 30:00:00 → 12:00:00
Call, Comp, Close at operator speed. Justin shadows. Live calls. Live "yeses." Driving to a property. Walking up to a door in Meta glasses. The clock keeps ticking visible in the corner. Stakes escalate: rejections → dead ends → near-miss → breakthrough.
05
The Handoff
Pipeline Delivered. Clock Hits Zero.
14:00 — 17:00 · CLOCK 00:00:00
Whether a contract lands or doesn't, by Saturday afternoon I've built him a real pipeline. Hand it over on camera. Justin to camera: what changes Monday. Me to camera: the leak most operators have. Plane back to Phoenix. Clock hits 00:00:00.
